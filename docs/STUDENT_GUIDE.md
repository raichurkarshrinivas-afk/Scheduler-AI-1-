# Scheduler - Student & Faculty Portal Guide

Welcome to the **Scheduler Student Portal**. This guide explains how to view your official class schedules, locate your examination seats, access offline copies, and submit inquiries or feedback.

---

## 1. Accessing Your Published Class Timetable

1. Open the Scheduler portal URL in any modern web browser on your phone, tablet, or computer.
2. Sign in with your student credentials or click **Public Timetables** if your institution allows open access.
3. Select your **Academic Term** and **Class / Cohort** (e.g., *Grade 10 - Section A* or *Computer Science - Year 2*).
4. The interactive timetable displays your weekly schedule:
   * **Subject / Course Name**
   * **Teacher / Instructor**
   * **Room Location** (e.g., *Science Lab 204*, *Main Auditorium*)
   * **Period Time** (e.g., *09:30 AM - 10:30 AM*)

### Color Coding
* **Lectures**: Slate Blue
* **Laboratory Sessions**: Emerald Green
* **Seminars / Tutorials**: Amber Gold
* **Breaks / Free Periods**: Light Gray

---

## 2. Examination Schedules & Seating Allocations

During official mid-term and final examination periods, your personalized exam schedule is available under **My Exams** (`/student/exams`):

### What Information is Displayed:
* **Subject Code & Title** (e.g., *MATH-301: Advanced Calculus*)
* **Date & Session** (e.g., *October 28, 2026 — Morning Session 09:00 - 12:00*)
* **Assigned Exam Hall** (e.g., *Examination Hall B - West Wing*)
* **Assigned Bench & Seat**:
  * **Bench Code**: e.g., `B-04-L` (Bench 4, Left Seat).
  * Clear directional indicators are displayed to help you quickly find your desk on the day of the exam.

### Alternating Seating Notice
To ensure academic integrity, seating arrangements stagger subjects across adjacent benches. Your bench neighbour will be taking a different exam subject.

---

## 3. Accessibility & Accommodations Privacy

> [!NOTE]
> **Privacy Notice on Accommodations**:
> If you have registered for accessibility accommodations (such as low-mobility ground floor seating, adaptive desk requirements, or quiet environment seating), the system automatically assigns an appropriate bench based on your institutional registry.
> **Your confidential accommodation details and medical notes are never shown on public door charts, student rosters, or peer screens.** Only your assigned seat code is visible.

---

## 4. Downloading Offline Timetables & Calendar Sync

* **Print / PDF Download**: Click the **Download PDF** button at the top right of your timetable to save an official, printable timetable card for your binder or offline storage.
* **Google Calendar Sync** (if enabled by your institution):
  * Click **Sync to Google Calendar**.
  * Authorize the calendar connection.
  * Your classes and exams will automatically appear as events on your phone's calendar, complete with room location reminders.

---

## 5. Submitting Feedback & Reporting Scheduling Clashes

If you discover a scheduling clash, have a question, or encounter an issue:

1. Click **Feedback & Support** in the navigation header or visit `/feedback`.
2. Choose a Category:
   * `Scheduling Conflict`: (e.g., two registered subjects scheduled at the same period).
   * `Access Issue`: (e.g., inability to locate exam hall or bench).
   * `General Question / Suggestion`.
3. Enter a clear subject and description of your issue.
4. Click **Submit**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Timetable Change Policy**:
> Submitting feedback is sent directly to the Academic Scheduling Coordinator for review.
> Feedback submissions do **not** immediately alter published timetables. If a change is approved, coordinators will publish a new revised version (`v1.1`), and an announcement notification will be posted to the portal.
